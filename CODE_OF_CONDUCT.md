# Community conduct

Treat contributors and users respectfully in issues, discussions, reviews and
other TMApps project spaces. Welcome different backgrounds and levels of
experience. Give specific, constructive feedback and keep disagreements focused
on the work. Make room for accessibility needs and clear communication.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexual harassment, spam and sharing
someone's private information are unacceptable. Do not publish personal account
data or credentials while reporting a problem.

Use GitHub's report-abuse controls for abusive content or contact the repository
maintainer through an existing appropriate private channel. Do not post sensitive
details in public to seek a contact method. No separate public conduct email is
currently designated. Security vulnerabilities use the repository's private
security-reporting form described in SECURITY.md.

Maintainers will assess reports in context, seek clarification where appropriate,
and may edit/remove content, limit participation or block repeated misconduct.
Respect privacy during assessment. Apply these expectations consistently,
including to maintainers, and avoid retaliation against good-faith reports.
