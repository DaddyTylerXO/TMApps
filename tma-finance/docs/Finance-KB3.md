# Finance-KB3: Your local profile

Applies to TMA-Finance 0.1.2 and later.

Select the circular profile icon in the top-right corner or the sidebar. Enter a display name and, optionally, an email address, then select **Save**. Your name appears beside the sidebar icon and its initial appears in the circular buttons. **Cancel** leaves the saved profile unchanged.

The profile is stored with the current local workspace and included in its JSON backup. Editing it does not create an online account, verify an email address, connect to a bank or sign in to the future shared market service. Demo and personal workspaces remain separate.
